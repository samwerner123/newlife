**what to publish** (for the season ahead) and **what's trending now**. Paths are relative to the live site.

Channels: Pinterest (pins from `marketing/pins/`), Instagram/TikTok/Reels (15–30 s "where to go in {month}" clips),
Facebook travel groups, X, and the newsletter once it exists.

## Weekly rhythm (suggested)

- **Pinterest:** 3–5 pins a day, rotating the three pin designs across the hubs in season.
- **Short video (1–2 a week):** "3 places with perfect weather in {month}" — use the top picks on `/where-to-go-in-{month}/`.
- **Facebook groups (1–2 a week):** answer "where should I go in…" questions with a genuinely useful reply; link only when it helps.
- **X / Threads (daily):** one data point a day from the site — for example the sunniest, warmest or cheapest pick of the month from a collection page, with the link.
