# Outreach templates

All templates are in English for US/UK partners. Replace `{…}` placeholders. Be personal and brief — mention a specific
article on their site.

## 1. Bloggers: free climate widget (backlinks)

Every destination page has an **"Embed this climate chart"** box with an iframe code; the widget links back to the
destination page.

> **Subject:** A free month-by-month weather widget for your {destination} guide
>
> Hi {name},
>
> I loved your post on {post title} — especially {specific detail}. Readers often ask when to visit, so I wanted to offer
> a free, embeddable weather chart for {destination}: average highs, lows and a month-by-month rating from official
> climate data. It's one line of code, loads fast and fits mobile:
>
> {iframe code from the destination page}
>
> Here's what it looks like: {link to /embed/{slug}/}. No strings attached — and I'm happy to make one for any other
> destination you cover.
>
> Thanks, {name}

## 2. Guest post pitch

## 3. Expert-source platforms (Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer and similar)

**Short bio:** "{Name} is the founder of {SITE name}, which rates 240+ travel destinations month by month using official
climate data, sea temperatures and seasonal risks like hurricanes and monsoons."

**Topics you can answer:** best time to visit {destination}, shoulder-season travel, hurricane/typhoon/monsoon season,
winter sun, ski seasons, where it's warm in {month}, travelling on a budget.

**Answer formula:** one clear recommendation → one number from the site → one practical tip → link to the relevant page.

## 4. Tourism boards and local operators

> **Subject:** Keeping our {destination} weather guide accurate

> Hi {name},
>
> Our page on {destination} ({link}) is visited by travellers deciding when to come. We use official climate normals for
> the weather and would love your help checking our festival dates and practical tips, or suggesting a better photo.
> We're also open to partnerships under our sponsored-content policy ({site}/advertise/).
>
> Thanks, {name}

## 5. Reddit, Quora and Facebook groups

- **Answer the question first.** Give the specific months, the reason (dry season, hurricanes…) and an alternative.
- **Link only when it adds something** (a month-by-month table, a comparison) — never more than once per thread.
- **Disclose** that it's your site ("I run a site that rates…").
- **Follow each community's rules;** many subreddits ban self-promotion entirely — then just answer helpfully.
