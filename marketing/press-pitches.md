# Data-story pitches for journalists

Travel editors love timely, data-backed lists. Each idea below is built on a page that already exists on the site, so the
numbers are always current — check the live page for the latest figures before you send a pitch. Pitch **6–8 weeks**
before the season (e.g. Christmas stories in late September–October).

## Story ideas

| Story | Hook / angle | Page with the data | Pitch window |
|---|---|---|---|
| The warmest places to spend Christmas | Ranked by real December climate normals, not marketing copy | `/christmas-destinations/` | Late Sept – Oct |
| Europe's best Christmas markets — and how cold they get | December highs and lows for 15 market cities | `/guides/christmas-markets-europe/` | Oct |
| Where you can ski in July (and every other month) | Southern-hemisphere and glacier skiing calendar | `/guides/ski-season/`, `/where-to-ski-in-july/` | May – June |
| Hurricane-season travel: the Caribbean islands that stay safest | Weather ratings Aug–Oct, ABC islands outside the belt | `/guides/caribbean-islands-compared/`, `/guides/hurricane-season/` | June – July |
| Sargassum-free beaches for summer | Which beaches face the seaweed and which don't | `/guides/sargassum-season/` | March – April |
| Perfect-weather destinations for every month of the year | One top pick per month from 240+ rated destinations | `/where-to-go-in-january/` … `/where-to-go-in-december/` | Any time (new-year round-ups in Dec) |
| Cheap places with great weather in January | Daily budgets + weather scores | `/cheap-places-to-travel-in-january/` | Nov – Dec |
| The world's sunniest beaches in winter | Sunshine hours, sea temperature and weather score | `/warm-places-to-visit-in-february/`, `/guides/winter-sun/` | Oct – Nov |
| Midnight sun: where the sun never sets this summer | Calculated midnight-sun dates by latitude | `/guides/midnight-sun/` | March – April |
| How warm is the sea? Swimming season by destination | Sea temperatures (2023–2025 averages) for 140 coasts | `/guides/diving-snorkelling-seasons/` + destination pages | March – May |

## Pitch e-mail template

> **Subject:** Data: the warmest places to spend Christmas {year} (ranked by climate normals)
>
> Hi {first name},
>
> With readers already planning their holidays, I thought this might be useful for a winter-sun or Christmas travel piece.
> We ranked {N} destinations by their real December weather — average highs, rainfall and seasonal factors like the
> Caribbean dry season — using 30-year climate normals from national weather services.
>
> A few findings:
> - {top destination} tops the list with average December highs of {x}°C ({y}°F)
> - {surprising pick} is the best value, from about ${z} a day
> - {myth-busting point, e.g. Bali is in its rainy season in December}
>
> Full ranking and methodology: {link}. Happy to send the data as a spreadsheet, provide a quote or create a custom cut
> (e.g. only destinations within 5 hours of New York or London).
>
> Best,
> {name}, {SITE name}

## Who to pitch

- Travel desks of national newspapers and their digital "travel inspiration" sections
- Travel magazines and newsletters (US and UK)
- Personal-finance writers (for budget angles)
- Local TV and radio travel segments before holidays
- Expert-source platforms (see `outreach.md`)

Keep a simple spreadsheet of who you pitched, when, the angle and the response; follow up once after a week.
